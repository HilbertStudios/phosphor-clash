Phosphor Clash

Privacy Policy · last updated 18 November 2026

Short version: Phosphor Clash is free and collects no personal data. No purchases, no accounts, no tracking, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs. Your save stays on your device (or in your own iCloud).

1. Who is responsible

Valentin Keckeisen
Email: hamham562@proton.me

2. What data the game processes

  • Save data (your cards, coins, decks and settings) is stored only locally: on your iPhone in the app's storage, or in the web version in your browser's local storage. It is never sent to us.
  • No data collection: the game has no user accounts and does not use tracking, advertising, analytics, profiling, crash-reporting or any third-party SDK. The game itself makes no network requests.
  • Game Center (iOS app, optional): friend duels and card trades use Apple Game Center. Only if you connect it, Apple shares your Game Center nickname with the friends you invite and delivers the small game message of each duel or trade (your public in-game profile: name, icon, level, showcase cards, deck or offered cards). We run no server and receive none of this data. Apple's privacy policy applies.
  • iCloud (iOS app, optional): if iCloud is enabled on your device, a copy of your save may be stored in your iCloud account by Apple so it can follow you to another device. We have no access to it. Backup files you export to iCloud Drive or Files are under your control. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.
  • No purchases: this version of the game has no in-app purchases and no paid content.
  • Web version on itch.io: the web version is hosted by itch.io. When you open the page, itch.io processes technical data (e.g. IP address in server logs) as the host, under its own privacy policy: itch.io/docs/legal/privacy-policy. The game itself collects nothing.

3. Legal basis

Because we do not collect or process any personal data ourselves, no legal basis under Art. 6 GDPR is required for our part. Processing by Apple (App Store, iCloud, Game Center) and itch.io (hosting) is done under their own responsibility.

4. Deleting your data

In the game: Settings → Delete save data. You can also delete the app (iOS) or clear the site data in your browser (web). iCloud data can be removed in your device's iCloud settings.

5. Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children, and has no purchases, ads or chat.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since we store no data about you, there is nothing on our side to access or delete – but feel free to contact us at any time.

7. Changes

If the game ever starts processing data (it currently does not), this policy will be updated before that happens.
